Most of what I know about my own research I learned by explaining it to someone
else, so I treat teaching and outreach as part of the work rather than beside it.

## Public astronomy

I present at the **W. J. McCallion Planetarium** at McMaster, running shows for
school groups and the general public. 

## Mentoring

I mentor students at several stages — undergraduate, MITACS summer, and
master's — on projects in time-series analysis and Maxwell-Bloch modelling. I also teach as a TA at McMaster, across Scientific Computing, introductory mechanics, quantum mechanics, and electricity and magnetism, and previously at the University of Tehran. 

## Community and service

I organize events for **Promoting Inclusivity in Physics &amp; Astronomy (PIPA)** at
McMaster — coffee hours, occasional movie nights, the small recurring things that make a
department feel like somewhere people want to be.
