Most of what I know about my own research I learned by explaining it to someone
else, so I treat teaching and outreach as part of the work rather than beside it.

I run shows at the **W. J. McCallion Planetarium** for school
groups and the general public, and I teach as a
TA at McMaster across Scientific Computing, introductory mechanics, quantum
mechanics, and electricity and magnetism, and taught previously at the University
of Tehran. I also mentor students on research projects — undergraduates, MITACS
summer students, master's students — on time-series analysis and Maxwell–Bloch
modelling.

The rest is the quieter sort of community work. I organize events for **Promoting
Inclusivity in Physics &amp; Astronomy (PIPA)** at McMaster: coffee hours, occasional
movie nights, the small recurring things that make a department feel like
somewhere people want to be. Reading groups are a long-standing habit of mine too
— at the University of Tehran I started and moderated a weekly feminist reading
circle, choosing the texts and keeping the discussion going from 2019 into 2020,
and I still look for reading groups wherever I end up.
